Where the per-note / once-only line goes · settled

Left of it, everything runs once per key you hold. Right of it, everything runs once. Big stripe makes that a place on the canvas; small box makes it just another module. This is the one switch that may be a real either/or rather than a setting — the stripe costs 150px of canvas forever. Settled: the stripe. The other two stay here only as a record of what was compared — they are not shipping, and this is not a setting.

Marking the feedback loop

CHORUS feeds back into DELAY. Loops are detected live — wire one without a Delay in it and it gets refused.

Where a refusal explains itself

Shipping defaults (#211): bézier cables, colour by meaning, cables drawn on top. Sag / swing / resting brightness ship at this page's values and get re-judged against the real Qt canvas.

Also live: LFO 1's note / glob switch moves it across the line and changes what it may modulate. Click a module to keep its cables lit. Click any cable to unplug it. Wire a loop with no Delay in it and it gets refused, with the whole cycle named.
