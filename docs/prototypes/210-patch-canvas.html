Prototype scaffolding Not proposed UI. These switches exist so you can compare the contested choices in one patch.

1 · Mix node treatment

Band is Massive X: the boundary is a place on the canvas. Box is a plain module — the boundary exists only where the cables meet it. Box + wash keeps the tinted halves and the edge labels but not the physical divider.

3 · Clutter controls

0.85

Reason's three graded hide modes, plus VCV's global opacity. Hover-to-reveal works in every mode, including Dots only — hover a jack or a knob. Dim unselected needs a click on a module first.

4 · Cable colour

per-note audio — left of Mix
once-only audio — right of Mix
modulation — ends on a knob

Squares carry audio, circles carry modulation — so a jack's shape says what may plug into it before you drag.

5 · Feedback loop

CHORUS → DELAY closes the cycle. Legal because a Delay module sits in it (Bitwig's rule, decision 56 §4).

6 · Refusal explanation

Drag OSC 1's Out onto DELAY's In: per-note into once-only. The input flashes; this switch decides where the sentence explaining it lands.

Also live: LFO 1's note/glob switch moves it across the boundary and changes what it is allowed to modulate — per-note LFO onto CHORUS's Rate is refused, global is not. Click a module to focus it (copper border, as today).
